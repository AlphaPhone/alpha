## Proposed end state
About 95 lines. Imports formatted and grouped. One `testMocks` read of `ELIZA_DEV_ALLOW_TEST_MOCKS`. The app version comes from a JSON import. The cloud-deployment check moves into an `apply:'build'` `buildStart` hook. The fixture swap no longer has the `existsSync` hole. The importer-allowlisted stub plugin and `disabledModuleSource` are replaced by one `@dev` alias chosen by the flag. The CSP and `build-flags.json` plugins stay byte-identical in output. `define` keeps `VITE_ELIZA_DEV_ALLOW_TEST_MOCKS`, `__APP_VERSION__` and `VITE_ALPHA_DEV_CARTESIA`. Aliases are built from the generated `aliases.json` in about 4 lines, and `@eliza-system` and `stated-clock-time` are gone. `fs.allow` keeps `apps/app`, the realpath of `.eliza/client-features` and `node_modules`, plus whichever vendor directories the dev server really needs.
