# Notes

The owner's notes are the resident agent's canonical Notes records (PRD AP-10, P-59): the phone reads
and edits them under the account it is bound to, keeps an unsaved edit on screen until a save is
confirmed, and sends a deletion to a Trash the owner can restore from. With the owner's three-day choice
the Trash erases a note after review or after three days, and a voice note's recording is erased on the
device only with the device's own proof. Voice notes are recorded on the phone and claimed once for their
note. The agent filters, records and organises only through reviewed device actions. Notes saved before
this version arrive once, through the agent's own import, as part of the same collection.
The folder list, the origin links and the earlier device Trash that the earlier Alpha kept in its
encrypted connection slots move into the phone's secure store, as spec/platform/storage.md moves a
legacy slot, before Notes first reads each of them. The earlier Alpha's device collection and its
unsaved changes remain in their original encrypted slots and can be reviewed in
[Previous notes](./previousDevice.md).
