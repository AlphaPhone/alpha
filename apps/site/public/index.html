Calendar

Ask about your day.

Alpha reads your calendar and answers in a sentence, with the events right there to tap.

Messages and calls

It asks before it acts.

Before it sends a text, places a call or changes an event, Alpha shows you exactly what it is about to do and waits for your yes.

Maps

Find a place without opening an app.

Answers arrive as a map and a short list, inside the conversation. Tap one to go.

Inbox

Catch up on mail with one question.

Alpha reads your Gmail and tells you what needs you. Then it drafts the replies for you to approve.

Voice

Or just say it.

Tap the mic and talk. Alpha listens, answers out loud and does the same work, hands free.

The agent lives on the phone.

Runs on your device

The agent, its memory and its database run on the phone itself. A cloud account supplies model inference, speech and Gmail.

Open source

The phone’s interface, its built-in apps and the agent are all on GitHub. Read them, change them, build your own.

You stay in charge

Alpha asks before it does anything that matters, and shows you what it is about to do.
